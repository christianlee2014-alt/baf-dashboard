About International Campus

Blooming Academies Foundation has set its sights on global expansion, bringing our interdisciplinary educational model to international communities. Our vision is to replicate the success of our Florida campuses on a global scale, adapting our K-12 programs and integrated clinical services to serve diverse populations worldwide while maintaining the high standards that define our foundation.
