K-12 Educational Services

Empowering Florida's Future

Blooming Academies Foundation supports Florida K-12 students, families, and schools with innovative, evidence-based learning that fosters growth and long-term academic success.

Programs & Pathways

Evidence-based programs that drive student achievement.

Academic Coaching

Empowering students with evidence-based coaching and personalized study habits that drive achievement across core Florida curricula.

Enrichment Labs

Dynamic spaces for hands-on learning in STEM and the arts, fostering creativity and critical thinking beyond the traditional classroom.

Foundation Support

Specialized literacy and numeracy programs designed to bridge achievement gaps and establish a strong base for future learning.

Supporting Educators

Empowering your teaching practice.

Empower your teaching practice with professional learning, personalized coaching, and model lessons tailored to Florida classrooms. We provide the tools and evidence-based frameworks that help teachers effectively implement the SEED/E.N.E.R.G.Y. learning model, ensuring educators have the resources to blossom alongside their students.

Student Support Services

Every student's journey is unique.

Evidence-Based Interventions

Our support services are designed to bridge learning gaps and build lasting confidence through evidence-based interventions. From targeted one-on-one tutoring to small-group labs and enrichment, we ensure that every child in Florida has the academic safety net they need to succeed.

Progress Monitoring

We utilize comprehensive progress monitoring systems that allow us to adapt learning strategies in real-time. We work intimately with families and school administrators to create a unified front for student achievement, fostering a collaborative environment where students blossom both in the classroom and at home.
