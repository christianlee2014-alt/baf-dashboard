Admissions

Admissions Requirements

We aim to make the enrollment process at Blooming Academies as seamless and supportive as possible for every student and family.

Enrollment Checklist

  • Eligibility: Proudly serving Florida students across all grade levels from Kindergarten through Grade 12.
  • Required Documents: Copies of academic transcripts, latest report cards, and immunization records should be submitted via our portal.
  • Specialized Support: Please include current IEP/504 documentation and any clinical or educational evaluations to ensure we can meet individual needs.
  • Important Deadlines: Priority applications for the 2026-2027 academic year are requested by May 15, 2026.
  • Paperwork Assistance: Our Admissions Team is available to provide personalized help with documentation and clinical record requests.

Orientation & Family Information Sessions

Admissions orientation information is vital for families preparing for the academic year. We invite you to join us for our upcoming sessions to meet our dedicated faculty, understand our community standards, and prepare your student for success.

FAQ

Enrollment & Admissions FAQs

How do I start the application process for my child?

All applications are submitted through our secure online portal. We suggest that families first schedule a campus tour and attend an orientation session to better understand our specialized K-12 environment and how we support unique student needs.

What is the typical enrollment and decision timeline?

Blooming Academies accepts applications year-round. While our main intake periods are for the Fall and Spring semesters, we offer rolling admissions if a space becomes available mid-term. Most families receive an enrollment decision within 14 business days after document submission.

Are there tuition fees or financial funding options available?

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and our goal is accessibility. We utilize a sliding scale for fees and provide comprehensive support to help families secure Step Up For Students or AAA Scholarships. Our internal financial aid office is available to discuss specific funding strategies with you.

What transportation options are available for students?

We provide regional bus services for students within a 15-mile radius. Routes are optimized twice a year based on our current student demographics to ensure safe and efficient transit for every family.

How do clinical therapies and educational services integrate?

Our interdisciplinary model bridges the gap between the clinic and the classroom. IEP goals and therapeutic strategies are embedded directly into daily lesson plans, allowing students to practice social and functional skills in a natural academic setting.

Who should I contact if I have further questions?

Our Admissions Team is here to help! Please reach out to our Enrollment Coordinator via email at info@bloomingacademies.org or by calling our office directly at (728) 231-2718 for any inquiries regarding records or paperwork.
