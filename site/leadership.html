Leadership

The Visionaries Behind Our Foundation

Our leadership team brings decades of experience in educational strategy, clinical services, nonprofit administration, and community development to ensure every Florida student has what they need to succeed.

EV

Dr. Elena Vance

Executive Director

Elena oversees the foundation's strategic vision and day-to-day operations, ensuring every initiative aligns with our mission of K-12 student success. With over twenty years of experience in educational leadership, she fosters partnerships with Florida school boards and community stakeholders. Her dedication to equity-driven growth has transformed the foundation into a regional leader for family empowerment.

MT

Marcus Thorne

Director of Administration

Marcus coordinates the organizational structure and logistical workflows that support our foundation's statewide initiatives. He manages personnel resources, facility operations, and administrative strategy to ensure a stable and efficient environment for our community programs. Marcus's focus on excellence enables our team to deliver consistent, high-quality educational support to students across Florida.

EV

Dr. Elena Vance

Senior Director of Academic Strategy, Curriculum & Instruction

Dr. Vance oversees the integration of innovative pedagogical frameworks and Florida educational standards across our entire network. She leads professional development initiatives that empower educators to deliver high-impact, personalized instruction for every K-12 student. Her strategic focus on curriculum design ensures academic rigor and creative engagement, preparing students for future success in higher education and leadership roles.

MS

Marcus Sterling, CPA

Controller / Director of Finances

Marcus provides strategic oversight of the foundation's financial health and scholarship endowments. He ensures rigorous fiscal accountability and transparent reporting, allowing our academic programs to scale effectively across Florida's K-12 landscape. His expertise in non-profit management guarantees that every donated dollar directly supports our mission of student empowerment.

SM

Dr. Sarah Mitchell

Director of Clinical Services

Dr. Sarah Mitchell oversees the coordination and delivery of clinical support services, ensuring a holistic approach to student well-being across all academies. She manages regional therapy programs and counseling initiatives that help K-12 students overcome emotional and developmental barriers to learning. Her leadership focuses on integrating health services with educational instruction to foster a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment for Florida's diverse student population.

EV

Elena Vance

Director of Recruitment, Enrollment and Retention

Driving the strategic growth of our academic community, this director oversees the complete lifecycle of engagement for both students and families. She develops innovative outreach programs and streamlined enrollment pathways to ensure that Florida's most promising students have access to our resources. By focusing on long-term retention strategies, she helps build a stable and thriving K-12 environment where every foundation member can blossom.
