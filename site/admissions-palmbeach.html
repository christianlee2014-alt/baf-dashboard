About Palm Beach Campus

Located at 125 S. State Rd 7, Wellington, FL 33414, our Palm Beach campus is the flagship location of Blooming Academies Foundation. We offer a full range of K-12 educational programs, integrated clinical services, and robust community partnerships designed to support every student's unique journey. Our interdisciplinary model bridges the gap between the clinic and the classroom, ensuring that therapeutic strategies are embedded directly into daily learning.
