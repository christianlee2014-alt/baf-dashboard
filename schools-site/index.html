School Network Overview

Where academic recovery, clinical support, and college-prep pathways come together.

Blooming Academies Foundation is building a Florida-based network of student-centered schools designed to serve families with rigor, structure, and wraparound support. BAF combines core academics, clinical services, special populations support, and community partnerships to create campuses where students can stabilize, grow, and graduate with momentum.

Current Network Snapshot

From Palm Beach to a broader Florida footprint.

BAF is positioned as a school network with one active Palm Beach launch model and planned growth into Broward and Tampa. Each location is structured around student support, operational consistency, and community-aligned expansion.

Palm Beach (BAF) Operating model based in Palm Beach County with integrated academics, therapy support, speech services, ESOL, and career-focused programming.
Broward Campus Planned Florida campus designed to extend the BAF model into Broward County with a larger student base and the same support-forward structure.
Tampa Campus Planned Tampa expansion intended to broaden regional impact and establish another campus under the same academic and student-services framework.
Blooming Academies Foundation operates with a mission-first approach: deliver strong academics, remove barriers to participation, and embed the services students need to stay engaged, supported, and on a path toward long-term success.
Why The Model Matters

A school network built around access, support, and measurable student progress.

The BAF model is designed for students who need more than a standard classroom experience. That means combining academics with clinical intervention, community-informed support, operational discipline, and a campus experience that can adapt to real family needs.

Core

Academic focus

ELA, math, science, and social studies remain the instructional spine, with support systems layered around student growth rather than separated from it.

Integrated

Clinical support

Therapy, speech, and specialized student services are treated as part of the learning environment, not an afterthought.

Flexible

Family-centered access

Transportation support, food service, and targeted interventions help reduce friction for students and families.

Scalable

Expansion-ready model

The same operational and service framework can be extended from Palm Beach into Broward, Tampa, and beyond.

Campus 01

Palm Beach (BAF)

Operating
Palm Beach County, FL Initial launch year: FY27 Foundational campus

Palm Beach is the lead BAF campus model and serves as the blueprint for the broader network. It combines college-preparatory academics with embedded clinical support, speech therapy access, ESOL services, special education support, and career-aligned programming.

  • Core academics in ELA, math, science, and social studies
  • Integrated therapy and speech support
  • ESL / ESOL and special education services
  • Career and technical education pathways
  • Transportation support through bus passes
  • Food service and student event programming

Student Outcomes

What success is expected to look like.

The dashboard already outlined the right public-facing themes: academic growth, retention, attendance, parent satisfaction, and clinical progress. Here they’re translated into website language families and partners can understand.

Academic Growth

BAF aims to build year-over-year gains in ELA, math, science, GPA, and the percentage of students performing at or above grade level.

Retention & Attendance

The model targets strong student retention, consistent daily attendance, lower absenteeism, and a more stable educational experience.

Clinical Progress

Clinical service delivery is intended to support behavioral improvement, IEP goal progress, and stronger day-to-day functioning for students who need it.

Family Satisfaction

Quarterly feedback and parent satisfaction are expected to be central indicators of whether the schools are meeting family needs well.

Student Engagement

Events, support touchpoints, and consistent service delivery are designed to improve school connection and follow-through.

Long-Term Readiness

The ultimate goal is not just short-term stabilization, but stronger readiness for graduation, postsecondary pathways, and workforce participation.
